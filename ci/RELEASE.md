**Take 203 — Hiking trails are no longer routed for ORVs; honest climbs, closures and darkness.**

Hiking-only trails (the Iron Belle, the North Country Trail, Shore-to-Shore
and about 10,500 miles more) could be chosen for a dirt bike or a quad. They
are now for walking only, and their card says "not an ORV trail". A trail
the DNR marks closed is never routed, on foot either, and its signed detour
is open.

Climb and drop on the route card follow the way you ride, and the
elevation profile shows true heights. A route started before sunrise says
so, and so does a river run that would end after sunset. A resumed loop
carries on as the same loop. Voice calls a re-route's first turn and does
not repeat a cue. Riding-area and campground cards say only what the data
holds.

On the phone: tap a hiking trail as a dirt bike: does its card say "not an
ORV trail"? With voice on, wander off a route: is the new route's first
turn called?
