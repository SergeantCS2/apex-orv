**Take 130 — pick a mode, don't cycle.**

The mode chip opens a picker: Ride, Outdoors, Water, each with a line
saying what it is for. Tap the one you want. Plus 129: trail-system pins,
county lines, hills from further out.

### Check on the phone
Tap the mode chip: three rows. Tap Water: the chip reads Water, the
picker closes, the map changes. Tap it again and choose Ride.
