### Everything that is new to your eyes (T1–T4, none of it seen on-device yet)

- **One dark control language** — every cream pill is gone; professional icons;
  orange appears exactly once per screen.
- **The bottom panel is a real sheet** — rounded top, grabber, and the place
  you tapped LEADS the card; coordinates demoted to the bottom.
- **Barlow everywhere** — the app chrome AND every map label, one typeface.
- **The light map is the AllTrails treatment**: green forest, the Huron NF
  boundary washed deeper, wetlands their own tone, white cased roads, blue
  water with blue names.
- **Hybrid is the onX treatment**: dimmed, desaturated imagery so the trails
  glow.
- **Category badges** — brown tent, green tree, blue boat, gold beach — with
  names in the matching colour. Same on river pins.
- **M-33 wears the Michigan diamond.**
- The Bull Gap / Mio row is gone — open **Search** with an empty box for the
  quick-jumps.
