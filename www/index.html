APEX all of Michigan

An offline map for riding, hiking, hunting and the water, both peninsulas. Everything works with no signal — the trails, the routing, the rivers and the photos are already on your phone.

Pick a mode

  • Off-road — ORV, dirt bike, side-by-side, MTB. Trails by difficulty, riding areas, fuel. What your machine may legally use.
  • Outdoors — hike, fish, explore. Hiking routes, trail systems, named hills and rivers. Routes on foot.
  • Hunt — 4.7 million acres of state land with boundaries, game areas, county lines, and stands, cameras and sign you mark.
  • Water — beach, kayak, tube, boat. Launches, beaches, marinas, lighthouses, rivers with float times. No trail lines.
  • Camp — campgrounds by type (state forest, national forest, private; rustic or modern; fee or free), the national and state forest land where dispersed camping is allowed, and supplies on the way in.

The mode chip is top-left. A mode is a starting point, never a cage: everything it sets is one tap away in Layers and the activity chip.

Getting around

  • Map — layers, search, find yourself
  • Plan — your machine, home, fuel range, loops, saved routes
  • Ride — start and stop recording; the ride sheet shows your trip, and what is left of a route
  • Tools — compass, mark a spot, diagnostics, this guide

The map

  • Tap a trail, road, pin or green land for its card. Major places carry a photo when one exists; the panel folds away when you tap empty ground.
  • Press and hold anywhere to drop a pin — then save it, make it home, or tap Route here, and Ride it on the route card.
  • Hybrid shows the photo of the state; every riding area is sharp to street level, the rest of Michigan is a broad view.

What it knows

  • Which trails your machine may legally use, from the DNR and the Forest Service — it will not route you somewhere you cannot ride. Closed trails are red and never routed through.
  • 4.7 million acres of state land — forest, game areas, parks — with their boundaries. Check the DNR for the rules of the unit before you hunt.
  • 76 rivers with launches, campgrounds, dams and portages. Pick two points for the float between them.

If something goes wrong

Dispatch gives you your position, the nearest junction and the nearest pavement, in words you can read aloud on a phone call. It refuses to guess: if it does not know where you are, it says so.

This guide lives under How to use on the Tools tab. It will not show again on its own.
